MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

Section 1. Purpose
The parties may exchange confidential information to evaluate a possible business relationship.

Section 2. Obligations
Each party will use reasonable care, disclose information only to representatives who need it, and use it only for the stated purpose.

Section 3. Exclusions
Information already public, independently developed, or lawfully received from another source is not confidential.

Section 4. Term
These confidentiality duties continue for three years after disclosure. Trade secrets remain protected while legally considered trade secrets.
