RESIDENTIAL LEASE — SAMPLE B

Section 1. Term
The lease begins January 1, 2027 and ends December 31, 2027.

Section 2. Rent
Tenant pays $1,925 on the first day of each month. A $100 late fee applies after three days.

Section 3. Renewal
The lease automatically renews for another 12 months unless Tenant gives 90 days written notice.

Section 4. Early termination
Early termination is permitted only with Landlord's written approval and payment of three months' rent.

Section 5. Disputes
All disputes must be resolved by binding arbitration.
