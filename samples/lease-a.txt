RESIDENTIAL LEASE — SAMPLE A

Section 1. Term
The lease begins January 1, 2027 and ends December 31, 2027.

Section 2. Rent
Tenant pays $1,850 on the first day of each month. A $75 late fee applies after five days.

Section 3. Renewal
The lease renews month-to-month unless either party gives 60 days written notice.

Section 4. Early termination
Tenant may end early with 60 days notice and a fee equal to two months' rent.

Section 5. Disputes
The parties will attempt mediation before filing a court action.
